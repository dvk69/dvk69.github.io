GliomaAI Research

Vineeth Kumar Deepala | Lead Researcher & Data Engineer

The Mission

GliomaAI is developing robust, automated brain tumor grading systems that remain accurate even when MRI scan data is incomplete—a frequent challenge in clinical settings worldwide[cite: 21, 79, 440].

Core Innovations

  • Modality Attention Fusion: Dynamically identifies the most informative MRI scans (T1, T1c, T2, FLAIR) for each specific patient[cite: 94, 95, 300].
  • Missing Modality Robustness: Maintains high diagnostic accuracy (0.876 AUC with only 2 scans) where standard models fail[cite: 115, 397, 438].
  • Uncertainty Quantification: Uses Monte Carlo Dropout to provide "Confidence Scores" (σ), alerting doctors when a case requires manual review[cite: 120, 128, 353, 408].

Research Validation

Validated on the BraTS 2021 gold-standard dataset (1,251 patients) from 18 international institutions[cite: 143, 145, 149, 650].

  • Full Model AUC: 0.914 (±0.011)[cite: 371, 372, 437].
  • Baseline Comparison: +2.4% improvement over the best single-modality approach[cite: 437, 454, 650].
