A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I like hard data problems
with real consequences.

I’m most interested in the point where a data system stops being a diagram and has to work for real people.

My background is in healthcare and insurance data engineering. I’ve worked with Spark, Databricks, Snowflake and AWS, but the tool is never the point. I care about whether the data arrives on time, whether people can trust it, and whether the next decision is better because of it.

I’m now pursuing a Doctor of Computer Science and using that work to go deeper into drift, fairness and dependable AI systems.
