PRODUCTION TRADEOFFS

Decisions at scale

DuckDB → cloud warehouse

Embedded analytics keeps the project portable; multi-team production workloads would move durable facts to Snowflake, BigQuery or Redshift.

Batch scoring → feature service

Precomputed affinity is predictable and inexpensive; high-velocity personalization would require streaming features and online model monitoring.

Local rules → governed policy

Shelf-life constraints are explicit and testable; enterprise deployment needs versioned policy ownership, audit logs and controlled overrides.
