PRODUCTION TRADEOFFS

Decisions at scale

Synthetic → governed clinical data

The demo avoids PHI completely; production needs IRB-approved access, schema contracts, lineage and controlled research workspaces.

Fixed baseline → rolling reference

A stable control improves interpretability; long trials require versioned windows and safeguards against contaminating the baseline.

Statistical flags → operational workflow

KS and PSI identify distribution change, not clinical causality. Production alerts need ownership, adjudication and documented resolution.
